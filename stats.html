Collected per screen, once a day: a random device number, city (or area rounded to ~10 km), Home/Mosque, layout and feature settings, app version, browser, operating system, device model where the browser reveals it, screen size, and the number of errors. Never collected: names, emails, exact location, IP addresses, error text. Screen names are opt-in. Daily records are deleted after 90 days; monthly totals are kept. Each screen can turn this off in Settings.
